# Stakeholder Recommendations

## Executive Summary

A phishing attack resulted in unauthorized access to an employee Microsoft 365 account and the exposure of approximately 5,000 customer email addresses.

Although no financial information was accessed, the incident created reputational, operational, and potential compliance risks for the organization.

Immediate corrective actions and long-term security improvements are recommended.

---

## Priority Recommendations

### Priority 1: Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

**Risk Addressed:** Credential theft and unauthorized account access

MFA should be enabled for all employee accounts. The absence of MFA allowed the attacker to successfully access the compromised account using only stolen credentials.

**Expected Benefit:**

- Reduced risk of account compromise
- Improved protection against phishing attacks
- Stronger access control security

---

### Priority 2: Conduct Phishing Awareness Training

**Risk Addressed:** Employee susceptibility to phishing attacks

Employees should receive regular training focused on:

- Identifying phishing emails
- Recognizing fraudulent login pages
- Reporting suspicious messages
- Safe credential handling practices

**Expected Benefit:**

- Reduced likelihood of successful phishing attacks
- Improved security awareness across the organization

---

### Priority 3: Improve Monitoring and Alerting

**Risk Addressed:** Delayed incident detection

Authentication activity should be monitored for:

- Unusual login locations
- Impossible travel events
- Repeated failed login attempts
- Large file downloads

**Expected Benefit:**

- Faster incident detection
- Reduced attacker dwell time
- Improved response capability

---

### Priority 4: Review Access to Customer Data

**Risk Addressed:** Excessive access to sensitive information

Customer information should only be accessible to employees who require it to perform their job responsibilities.

**Expected Benefit:**

- Reduced exposure of sensitive information
- Improved data protection

---

### Priority 5: Develop an Incident Response Playbook

**Risk Addressed:** Inconsistent incident handling

The organization should maintain documented procedures for responding to:

- Phishing attacks
- Account compromises
- Data exposure incidents
- Malware infections

**Expected Benefit:**

- Faster response
- Consistent decision making
- Reduced business impact

---

## Long-Term Security Improvements

The following long-term improvements should also be considered:

- MFA enforcement across all systems
- Quarterly phishing simulations
- Regular security awareness training
- Periodic access reviews
- Security logging and monitoring enhancements
- Data loss prevention controls
- Vendor security assessments

---

## Final Recommendation

The highest priority action is the implementation of multi-factor authentication.

Had MFA been enabled, the attacker would have been significantly less likely to successfully access the employee account after obtaining the stolen credentials.

Combining MFA, employee awareness training, and improved monitoring would substantially reduce the likelihood and impact of similar incidents in the future.

# Phishing Incident Response Investigation

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates a simulated incident response investigation involving a phishing attack that resulted in account compromise and unauthorized access to customer information.

The investigation follows a structured incident response process to identify the attack, contain the threat, assess business impact, document evidence, and recommend corrective actions.

---

## Scenario

An employee received a phishing email impersonating Microsoft 365 and entered company credentials into a fraudulent login page.

An external attacker used the stolen credentials to access the employee account and download a spreadsheet containing approximately 5,000 customer email addresses.

No payment information or financial records were accessed.

---

## Incident Response Phases

### 1. Identification

The organization detected suspicious login activity associated with an employee account.

### 2. Containment

The affected account was secured to prevent additional unauthorized access.

### 3. Eradication

The phishing source and any persistence mechanisms were removed.

### 4. Recovery

Normal account operations were restored and additional monitoring was implemented.

### 5. Post-Incident Recommendations

Security controls were reviewed and improvements were recommended.

This project demonstrates how phishing attacks can result in unauthorized access to business systems and customer information. It highlights the importance of MFA, security awareness training, monitoring, and incident response procedures.
